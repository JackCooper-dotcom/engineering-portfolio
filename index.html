About

A bit about me

I'm a mechanical engineering student at the University of Maryland's A. James Clark School of Engineering. I enjoy engineering because of the problem-solving nature of it. I love thinking through different solutions and testing different methods before finally achieving my goals. One day, I hope that my contributions in engineering will make a real and positive difference in the world.

Most of what I know, I've learned by building things hands-on: modeling mechanical assemblies, working through statics and design constraints, and troubleshooting robotics projects when they don't work the way I thought they would. I'm still early in my engineering career, but I'm genuinely curious about how and why things work, and I like the process of getting a design from a sketch to something physical.

Outside of engineering, I enjoy many other things. I watch or play sports almost every day and try to stay active whenever I can. I love being out in nature and playing my guitar. I also try to spend as much time as I can with my two dogs, Frankie and Rocky.
